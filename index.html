What we're building toward

Three commitments guide every program we run, from a village wheelchair repair to a national policy meeting.

Vision

A Nepal where children with disabilities live with the same dignity, independence, and welcome as any other child.

Mission

To give every child access to a rehabilitation-friendly school, timely medical care, and steady counselling support.

Goal

To widen free access to education and healthcare so disability stops being a shortcut to poverty in Nepal.

Our core programs

Education, healthcare, rehabilitation, and skills training for children with disabilities — and the families raising them.

Emergency response

Earthquake & disaster relief

When the ground shifts, disabled children are the last to be evacuated and the first to be forgotten. We run rapid-response teams that reach them first — with medicine, mobility aids, and shelter built for their needs.

Learn more
Community safety

Flood & landslide preparedness

We map at-risk households with disabled children ahead of monsoon season, run evacuation drills adapted for wheelchair users, and keep emergency contact lines open through our district coordinators.

Learn more

CDCA activities

Sensitization campaigns on disability awareness, waste management, and women's health in the community.

Wheelchair repair & maintenance

Ongoing repair services for mobility devices distributed across Nepal, free of charge.

Tailoring training for women

Vocational training that builds a sustainable income for economically vulnerable families.

Non-formal education

Literacy and life-skills classes for women and children who missed formal schooling.

Medical treatment & support

Coordinated care with partner hospitals for children with physical disabilities.

Scholarship programs

School scholarships for children with disabilities in Kathmandu and beyond.

Psycho-social counselling

Awareness programs that work to change negative perceptions about disability.
